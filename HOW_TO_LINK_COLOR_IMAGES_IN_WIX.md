# How to Link Color Images to Products in Wix
### Out of Jersey — one-time setup per product

When this is done, customers on the website will automatically see the correct color photo when they click a color swatch on a product page. This takes about 5–10 minutes per product.

---

## What you need before you start
- The product photos for each color (on your computer or already uploaded to Wix Media)
- Access to your Wix dashboard

---

## Step-by-step

### 1. Open the product
1. Log in to your **Wix dashboard**
2. Go to **Store Products** (left sidebar)
3. Click on the product you want to set up (e.g. "40oz Tumbler with Handle")

---

### 2. Go to the Options tab
1. Scroll down to the **"Options & Variants"** section
2. You'll see your **Color** option listed with all the color names (Black, White, Blue, Red, etc.)

---

### 3. Link each color to its photo
For each color in the list:

1. **Click the color name** (e.g. "Orange")
2. A small panel will open — look for **"Add image"** or a photo icon next to that color
3. Click it and either:
   - **Upload** the photo from your computer, or
   - **Choose** a photo already in your Wix Media library
4. Select the correct photo for that color and click **Done**
5. Repeat for every color

> **Tip:** You only need to do this once per product. After that, the website handles everything automatically.

---

### 4. Save
Click **Save** at the top right when you're done. The website will update within a minute or two.

---

## Which products need this done?

Any product that has a **Color** option with multiple choices. Start with your most popular ones:

- [ ] 40oz Tumbler with Handle
- [ ] 30oz Tumbler with Handle  
- [ ] 20oz Tumbler
- [ ] Any other product with color variants

---

## What happens on the website once it's done

- Customer clicks the **Orange** swatch → main photo instantly switches to the orange version
- Customer clicks **Purple** → photo switches to purple
- The photo they see matches exactly what they'll receive
